Haushalt

Shopping list, chores and recipes for couples, families and flatmates, shared locally without a cloud.

Coming soon to Google Play

What the app does

  • Shopping list sorted by aisle and store, with voice input
  • Recurring chores, rotation and a waste-collection calendar with reminders
  • Recipe book, cooking mode with timers and a weekly meal plan that fills the shopping list
  • Share via QR code directly between devices on the same Wi-Fi, widgets for home screen and watch

Your data stays yours

  • No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your lists, chores and recipes stay on your devices.
  • Sharing happens only directly between your devices on the same Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted.

All details are in the privacy policy.
