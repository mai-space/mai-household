Haushalt brings shopping, chores and cooking together in one app – for couples, families and flatshares. No account, no cloud, no ads.

SHOPPING
• One shared shopping list for everyone in the household
• Items are sorted into the right aisle automatically (fruit & veg, dairy, drinks …) – and the app remembers when you move something
• Stores such as Aldi, HIT, Rewe and Netto, or your own, as filters: “oat milk only at Rewe”
• Store mode: shows only what that store has, in the order you walk through it – the order learns from what you tick off
• Voice input: “Two litres of milk, eggs and washing-up liquid at Rewe” becomes three entries with amount and store

CHORES
• Vacuuming, cleaning the bathroom, changing the bedding … “every 7 days after it was last done”, fixed weekdays or one-off
• Freshness bars instead of red overdue counters
• Assign chores to someone or take turns
• “Who did what” shows fairly how the work is shared
• Bin calendar for general waste, organic, paper, packaging and glass – also importable from an .ics calendar file, with a reminder the evening before

KITCHEN
• Recipe book with collections, favourites, tips and family stories
• Paste a whole recipe as text – ingredients and steps are recognised
• Scale servings, cooking mode with step-by-step view and timers
• Meal plan: what’s for dinner when? Put the week’s ingredients on the shopping list with one tap

TODAY
At a glance: chores due today, bins for tomorrow, today’s meal and the state of the shopping list.

WIDGETS & WATCH
• Home screen widgets for chores, shopping list and meal plan – freely resizable, tick off directly
• Wear OS watch: tiles, complications and a small list to tick off on your wrist

SHARED – WITHOUT A CLOUD
Sharing works with a QR code: scan once, then devices on the same Wi-Fi sync directly and encrypted (AES-256-GCM) while the app is open. There is no server and no account – your lists stay with you. People without their own phone, such as children, can be added to the household too.

BACKUP
Create a password-encrypted backup file and restore it on a new phone – the household reconnects without a new QR code.

PRIVACY
• All data stays on your device and the devices you share with
• The database on your device is encrypted
• No sign-up, no ads, no analytics
• Microphone only when you tap for voice input, camera only for scanning the QR code
• Reminders are sparing: one daily summary and the bins the evening before – nothing else

The app speaks German and English, following your system language.
